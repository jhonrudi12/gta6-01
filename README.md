# Solmar City: Streets of the Sun

[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/jhonrudi12/gta6-01/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)
[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/jhonrudi12/gta6-01/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)

An open-world crime game in a sun-soaked fictional beach city. Play as Marco Solis,
fresh back in Solmar City, working three jobs for fixer Lena Cruz: steal a car for her
garage, run a timed drop on the bayfront, and clear the rival crew off the harbour dock.

**The world:** a full street grid with towers, palm trees, a beach and the sea;
14 traffic cars, 24 pedestrians, patrol cars and foot officers who answer your wanted
level (0–5 stars), street cash and health pickups, and a 2D minimap.

**Play:** on foot (WASD, Shift to run, Space to jump, mouse drag to look) or behind
the wheel (steal any traffic car with E, F to get out). Fists and a pistol (1 / 2,
click to fire), missions with a 3D beacon and HUD objective, wanted heat that fades
when you lie low, and a BUSTED screen with a hospital respawn when your HP hits zero.

**Tech:** three.js (vendored), zero network requests, synthesised WebAudio sounds only,
progress saved in localStorage. Serve `game/` statically and open `index.html`.
